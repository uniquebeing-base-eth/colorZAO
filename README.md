# 🎨 ColorZAO

> Color your way through the ZAO ecosystem.

ColorZAO is a discovery-first mini app that transforms exploring the ZAO ecosystem into an interactive art experience.

Instead of scrolling through lists of projects, users reveal original artworks by painting grayscale canvases. Every completed canvas uncovers something new—a project, workshop, or creative fact—before inviting the community to leave meaningful feedback.

---

## ✨ Why ColorZAO?

Discovery shouldn't feel like browsing a directory.

It should feel like walking through a gallery.

Every canvas is a surprise.

Paint.

Reveal.

Discover.

Share your thoughts.

Repeat.

---

## 🖌️ Core Experience

1. Launch ColorZAO
2. Start painting a grayscale artwork
3. Reveal the hidden exhibit
4. Learn about a project, workshop, or creative fact
5. Smash or Pass
6. Explain why
7. Continue to the next canvas

---

## 🎨 Discovery Types

### 🚀 Projects
Discover builders shipping products across the ZAO ecosystem.

### 🎓 Workshops
Explore educational sessions and creator workshops.

### 💡 Creative Facts
Learn interesting facts about music, art, creativity, and the ZAO ecosystem.

---

## 🏛 Gallery Experience

Every exhibit appears randomly.

No two discovery sessions are exactly the same.

The artwork itself is original to ColorZAO and acts as the gateway to discovery. The paintings are intentionally unrelated to the projects they reveal, making every uncovering feel like a surprise.

---

## 💬 Community Signal

After revealing an exhibit, users can leave simple but meaningful feedback.

- 🔥 Smash
- 👎 Pass

If they vote, they're encouraged to explain why.

Feedback can be submitted:

- Anonymously
- With a Farcaster profile

Builders receive qualitative insights instead of just numbers.

---

## 🏆 Hall of Fame

Community favorites automatically rise through the rankings.

Projects and workshops earn their place based on genuine community feedback rather than paid promotion.
